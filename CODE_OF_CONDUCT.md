# Code of Conduct

Helmsman contributors are expected to keep discussions technical, specific, and respectful.

## Expected Behavior

- Assume good faith while still asking for evidence.
- Critique code, docs, and decisions directly.
- Keep PR discussions scoped to the change under review.
- Respect maintainer decisions about product direction, release timing, and safety boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private credentials, transcripts, or user data.
- Reintroducing removed unsafe surfaces after a maintainer has rejected them.
- Repeatedly derailing issues or PRs away from the technical decision.

Maintainers may edit, close, hide, or block participation when needed to keep the project usable.
