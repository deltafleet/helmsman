# Charting Loop

## Loop Rule

Charting is a recursive route-sharpening loop, not a single clarification pass.

```text
Signal Read -> Aperture Question Bundle -> Scoped Memory Scan -> Research Lanes -> Synthesis -> Sharpness Check -> loop or Route Lock
```

## Cycles

| Cycle ID | Starting Route Hypothesis | Question Bundle | User Answer | Memory Scan Result | Research Lanes | Synthesis | Sharpness Check | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C-001 | <route hypothesis> | question-bundles.md#c-001 | <answer source> | memory-scan.md#c-001 | <lanes or none> | <route update> | <lock-ready|loop|blocked and why> | <loop|lock|blocked> |
